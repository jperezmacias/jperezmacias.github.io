Bibliography & References

6. Bibliography and References

  1. 3D Sound & CIPIC HRTF Database:
    CIPIC Interface Laboratory - UC Davis
    Algorithmic research on Head-Related Transfer Functions and psychoacoustic localization.

  2. The Csound Reference Manual:
    Barry Vercoe et al., MIT Media Lab. Comprehensive reference guide for Csound opcodes, language syntax, and DSP architecture.

  3. Spatial Audio and Virtual Acoustics:
    Begault, Durand R. 3-D Sound for Virtual Reality and Multimedia. Academic Press, 1994.

  4. Head-Related Transfer Functions and Acoustic Measurement:
    Gardner, William G., and Keith D. Martin. “HRTF Measurements of a KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone.” MIT Media Lab Perceptual Computing Technical Report #280, 1994.

  5. Acoustic Simulation in Rooms:
    Allen, J. B., and D. A. Berkley. “Image method for efficiently simulating small-room acoustics.” Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, 65(4), 943-950, 1979.

  6. The Haas Effect (The Precedence Effect):
    Haas, Helmut. “The influence of a single echo on the audibility of speech.” Journal of the Audio Engineering Society, 20(2), 146-159, 1972.
